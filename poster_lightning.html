Proximity to Golf Courses and Housing Prices Research Poster

Quick overview of my research poster

In this spatial analysis project I used ModelBuilder to create an easy to follow workflow. This project's workflow is as follows:

1. Join Maricopa County, Arizona census tract data and golf courses.

2. Add centroids to each census tract and find the nearest golf courses.

3. Perform regression (mmregression toolbox)
